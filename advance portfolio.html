ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT

Farmers to Consumer Direct Platform

Concept and System Design by S. Mukesh

Problem Statement

Conventional agricultural supply chains involve multiple layers of middlemen, resulting in farmers receiving less than 30% of end-consumer price while urban consumers pay exorbitant rates for stale produce.

Technological Solution

  • Geo-Clustering: Algorithmic matching of harvest lots with nearby consumer hubs to minimize transportation waste.
  • Transparent Pricing Engine: Dynamic daily market quotes based on demand-supply indices, guaranteeing high farmer margins.
  • Direct Communication: Voice and text channels tailored for regional languages to ensure maximum accessibility.

Current Stage

Feasibility modeling, user flow mapping, and backend computational logic design in Python.
